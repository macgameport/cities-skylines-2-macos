# Code of conduct

This is a small project where people compare notes on getting games they own to run on a Mac. These
rules keep it useful.

**Expected.** Be civil and specific. Assume the other person is trying to help. Say what you ran, on which
Mac, and what happened. Disagree about the evidence, not about the person.

**Not accepted.**

- Harassment, insults or slurs, and sexual language or imagery.
- Posting anyone's private information.
- Asking for or sharing pirated games, licence keys, or ways around a game's ownership or licence checks.
  This project is for games you own.

**Where it applies.** Issues, Discussions, pull requests, and anywhere else someone speaks for the project.

**What happens.** A maintainer may edit, hide or remove a contribution, and may block someone who keeps at it.

**Reporting.** Use GitHub's own "Report content" link on the comment or profile. To reach the maintainers
privately, use the repository's Security tab, choose "Report a vulnerability", and say it is a conduct
report.
